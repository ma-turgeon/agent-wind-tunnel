The extra loop did not earn its place in one narrow comparison.

One synthetic scenario compared direct with loop under one OpenAI model/configuration, with three repetitions each. All six runs passed the local structural/lexical rubric. After that rubric tie, direct was selected for lower complexity. The sample used nine model calls, 12,019 tokens, zero retries, and a USD 0.08756 Price Card estimate.

The mechanism

Architecture becomes an experiment, not a diagram contest.

Topology is declared up front. Static validity and hard gates decide which candidates are admissible. The surviving design with the least structural burden is selected and packaged with the exact route that earned the decision.

1

Declare

Scenario, requirements, SLOs, budgets, trusted Policy, and candidate Blueprints.

2

Validate

Closed contracts, finite routes, capability scope, and approval dominance.

3

Execute

Exact node visits, edges, attempts, retries, joins, approvals, and terminals.

4

Gate

Conformance, reliability, authority, calls, tokens, latency, and planned spend.

5

Select

Pareto prune, prefer least complexity, ablate the selected family, retain evidence.

Enterprise portfolio case

Nine fictional initiatives, two explicit evidence partitions.

The enterprise pack connects agent architecture to portfolio intake, capacity, privacy, security, approval, evidence, procurement, service desk, and eligibility decision support. Its sources are public governance frameworks; its facts remain fictional composites.

Calibration · 6 cases

All four mechanisms are exercised

Direct wins intake completeness. Loop wins capacity ranking and evidence-gap resolution. Parallel review wins data-sharing risk and dependency review. The governed graph wins high-impact approval routing.

Separated · 3 cases

Still synthetic composite evidence

Parallel review wins procurement copilot selection. Loop wins service-desk pilot. The governed graph wins eligibility decision support. This file is held apart from calibration, but it is not external or human-labelled validation.

Both packs select graph-max-governed as the smallest one canonical candidate covering every case in that pack. That universal choice does not mean every individual case should use it.

The repository includes an optional blinded-review kit and a blank review sheet. No completed sheets, calibrated human labels, or reviewer-agreement result are claimed.

Proof before pitch

What another reviewer can falsify.

Implementation

Closed and bounded

Unknown routes, unbounded cycles, unsupported contracts, and forged capabilities fail validation or execution.

Authority

Policy stays outside the graph

Initial grants are immutable. A consequential simulated effect requires an exact run- and artifact-bound approval.

Reproducibility

Evidence is inspectable

Fixture outputs are byte-stable. A manifest verifier checks membership, byte counts, and SHA-256 hashes.

Economics

Spending is explicit

Live plans require call and spend ceilings. Versioned exact-model Price Cards remain attached to estimates.

Failure evidence

Ablations must break something important

A graph component is retained only when a declared important failure returns after removal.

Honesty

Evidence modes remain separate

Deterministic fixture, separated composite, live empirical, and production evidence support different claims.
